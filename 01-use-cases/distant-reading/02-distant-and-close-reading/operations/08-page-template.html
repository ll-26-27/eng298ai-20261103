Scenes of instruction, beyond word search

Where in these four texts does one being teach, train, or form another? A keyword search can only find the words teach, learn, lesson. Here a model read every passage and judged whether instruction was happening, whatever the words. Below, the two methods side by side across whole texts (distant), a hand check of how often the model is right, and the strongest passages read closely.

Why this concept: Lord Henry remaking Dorian, the creature learning speech by watching the De Laceys, Socrates turning Phaedrus toward philosophy, and the last Robots begging Alquist to teach them how Robots are made are all scenes of instruction, and almost none of them announce themselves with the word "teach". Wilde's word for it is influence; Shelley's creature calls language "a godlike science".
