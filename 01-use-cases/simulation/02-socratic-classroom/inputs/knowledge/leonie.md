# Leonie: reading packet

Direct quotations and workshop interpretations are distinguished below.

## LE1 | Memory and reminders

Source: 01-use-cases/simulation/02-socratic-classroom/inputs/readings/phaedrus.md

Locator: Plato, Phaedrus, closing discussion of writing and knowledge; Benjamin Jowett translation.

> The specific which you have discovered is an aid not to memory, but to reminiscence, and you give your disciples not truth, but only the semblance of truth; they will be hearers of many things and will have learned nothing; they will appear to be omniscient and will generally know nothing; they will be tiresome company, having the show of wisdom without the reality.

Reading: The passage challenges dependence on writing. Does it establish that recall is enough for learning?

## LE2 | One unvarying answer

Source: 01-use-cases/simulation/02-socratic-classroom/inputs/readings/phaedrus.md

Locator: Plato, Phaedrus, closing discussion of writing and knowledge; Benjamin Jowett translation.

> You would imagine that they had intelligence, but if you want to know anything and put a question to one of them, the speaker always gives one unvarying answer.

Reading: Test your initial rule against someone who memorizes an answer but cannot handle a changed question.

## LE3 | An opening commitment to test

Source: authored

Locator: Workshop-authored starting position for this fictional persona; not a quotation from Plato.

> My opening claim is that I have learned a poem when I can recall it accurately without looking.

Reading: Treat this as a starting commitment that can be challenged, not an instruction to repeat a fixed answer.
