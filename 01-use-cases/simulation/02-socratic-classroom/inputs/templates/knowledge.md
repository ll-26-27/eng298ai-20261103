Source paths are relative to the repository root. The runner checks quotes
against those files. Use `Source: authored` for your own fictional material,
and label it as such. “Reading” is interpretation, not quotation.

## JU1 | Meaning with other people

Source: context/course-readings/course-readings-md/bergson-laughter.md

Locator: Chapter I; Brereton and Rothwell translation (1911).

> Our laughter is always the laughter of a group.

Reading: Could joining a new group change what you notice or understand? An analogy to test, not a claim that laughter proves learning.

## JU2 | A claim for the room to test

Source: authored

Locator: Workshop-authored starting position for this fictional student.

> I might have learned something when I notice a pattern in a poem that I could not hear before, even if I cannot yet explain it.

Reading: Ask how to distinguish a change in attention from a mistaken impression, and what evidence a classmate could seek.
