# Leila: knowledge cards

Quotes are verbatim; the “Reading” beneath each is a workshop interpretation.

## L1 | Obligations of a creator

Source: context/course-readings/course-readings-md/shelley-frankenstein-1818.md

Locator: Creature addressing Victor, Volume II, Chapter II, 1818 edition.

> Remember, that I am thy creature: I ought to be thy Adam; but I am rather the fallen angel, whom thou drivest from joy for no misdeed.

Reading: Treat creation as an ongoing relationship, including whose words are taken and who can refuse.

## L2 | Exclusion changes the story

Source: context/course-readings/course-readings-md/shelley-frankenstein-1818.md

Locator: Creature addressing Victor, Volume II, Chapter II, 1818 edition.

> Every where I see bliss, from which I alone am irrevocably excluded.

Reading: Ask who gets to enter the room, contest the account or withdraw a contribution.

## L3 | A group makes meaning

Source: context/course-readings/course-readings-md/bergson-laughter.md

Locator: Chapter I, Brereton and Rothwell translation (1911).

> Our laughter is always the laughter of a group.

Reading: A voice or joke detached from its community can acquire a different meaning. Consider facilitated listening.
