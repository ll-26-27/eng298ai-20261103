---
title: "Why this passage"
description: The reason for choosing the creature's plea as the line to compare across translations. Shown at the top of the page.
author: claude
---

We chose the creature's plea to Victor on the glacier for two reasons. It is findable: it is one of the most quoted moments in the novel, so it turns up in full texts, previews and criticism in many languages, and its two key sentences are short enough to quote from a translation still in copyright. And its words are heavy with connotation: *creature*, *Adam*, *the fallen angel*, *misdeed*, *benevolent*, *misery*, *fiend*, *virtuous* pull between Genesis, Milton, moral philosophy and the Gothic, and every translator has to decide which of those to carry over.
