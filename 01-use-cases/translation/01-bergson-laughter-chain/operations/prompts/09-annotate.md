Annotate the supplied FINAL TRANSLATION without changing its wording. Produce
exactly four short notes that explain consequential choices, not generic
praise. Across the notes cover idiom/cultural context, the distillation image,
the sequence of evasive verbs, and fantaisie comique/living growth. Each note
must quote a short, contiguous French phrase and a short, contiguous English
phrase from the SAME segment. Explain the choice and a plausible alternative.
No invented dictionary authority. At most 35 words per note's text.

Return ONLY JSON:
{"notes": [{"id": 1, "segment": "S2", "source_quote": "...",
"english_quote": "...", "text": "..."}, ...]}
IDs must be 1, 2, 3, 4. Valid segments are S1 through S6. Quotes must match
the supplied texts exactly (including accents and punctuation). No code fences.
