# Read the argument and its movement

Use the source and source card to give short notes under Author/work,
Argument, Rhythm, and Translation priorities. Distinguish facts supplied by
the source record from your interpretation. Notice the four opening questions,
the accumulation of evasive verbs, and living growth versus enclosing a
definition. This is prose: do not invent a meter or a phonetic transcription.
At most 200 words. Do not use the published English translation yet.
