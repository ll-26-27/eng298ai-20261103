# Critique against the French

Compare the CURRENT DRAFT directly with the original French. Identify up to
four specific strengths or problems in a table: segment, exact French phrase,
exact draft phrase, diagnosis, proposed action. Separate mistranslation from
an optional stylistic preference. Retain sound choices; do not invent a flaw
merely to fill the table. At most 230 words. This is the same model reviewing
its work, not an independent scholarly validation.
