# Editorial brief

Translate for humanities students reading Bergson for the first time. Keep the
four opening questions, the distillation metaphor, the sequence of evasive
movements, and the contrast between definition and living growth. Use clear
contemporary English. Avoid silently importing the 1911 translator’s idioms.

For the last editing stage: preserve the rhythm where it remains readable;
explain “fantaisie comique” and “vaudeville” in notes rather than expanding the
translation into a paraphrase. If fidelity and fluency conflict, name the
trade-off. Do not assume the published comparison is the only correct reading.

This is the prepared workshop brief. Change it to make your own editorial
intervention visible; it is not evidence of a student’s completed review.
