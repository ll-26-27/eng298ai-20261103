**Author/work**
- *Record:* Henri Bergson (1859–1941), *Le Rire*, ch. I opening. The French is Alcan 1938, via Wikisource, with six editorial segments.
- *Not verified here:* the transcription against the scan. I give no first-publication date because the record gives none.

**Argument** (my interpretation)
- Four questions (S1 has two, then S2 and S3) move from the problem, to a range of examples running from grimace to fine comedy, to a distillation image that assumes one constant essence.
- S4 says the problem resists even great thinkers.
- S5–S6 change approach: Bergson will not enclose the comic in a definition but will treat it as living and watch it grow.

**Rhythm**
- The questions build from short to long.
- S4 stacks four evasive verbs without conjunctions (*se dérobe, glisse, s'échappe, se redresse*). The last is a reversal, followed by the sharp appositive *impertinent défi*.
- S5–S6 are calmer, with repeated *Nous* and a closing pair, *grandir et s'épanouir*.

**Translation priorities**
- Keep four real questions.
- Keep the distillation image and the scent contrast.
- Keep four distinct verbs. *Se redresse* is the hardest.
- Keep the contrast between *enfermer/définition* and *vivant/grandir*.
- Explain *fantaisie comique* and *vaudeville* in notes.
- Trade-off: vivid verbs against abstract philosophical tone. My lexicon options are unverified.
