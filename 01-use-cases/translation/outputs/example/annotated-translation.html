Four choices to inspect

1
quiproquo de vaudeville → a vaudeville mix-up

Vaudeville here is the French genre of light stage comedy built on intrigue; keeping it preserves the cultural label. A plainer alternative, 'farce's mistaken-identity gag,' clarifies for Americans but loses the genre name.

2
Quelle distillation nous donnera l’essence → What distillation will give us the essence

Keeping 'distillation' literally preserves the image of one constant essence yielding many scents; 'intrusive' renders indiscrète as a smell lacking manners. Alternative: 'What process of extraction' would be plainer but lose the chemistry.

3
se dérobe sous l’effort, glisse, s’échappe, se redresse → gives way under the effort, slips off, escapes, springs back up

Four unconnected verbs keep the sequence of evasions; 'gives way under' suggests ground yielding. 'Springs back up' approximates se redresse. Alternative: 'eludes, slides, escapes, rights itself' sounds more philosophical but less vivid.

4
enfermer la fantaisie comique dans une définition → confine the comic fantasy within a definition

Fantaisie comique means playful comic imagination, set against definition and 'something living.' 'Fantasy' may suggest a genre; 'comic fancy' or 'whimsy' would stress play, though whimsy risks sounding trivial.
