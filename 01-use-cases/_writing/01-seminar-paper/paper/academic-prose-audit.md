## Problems corrected

- **Repeated abstractions:** the earlier draft repeatedly announced relationships among responsiveness, persistence, and answerability. The rewrite makes these distinctions through the rules of twenty questions and Grice's named examples.
- **Loose textual attribution:** the rewrite compares Jowett's “speaker” with Fowler's “written words,” rather than attributing a feature of one translation directly to Plato.
- **An inference larger than its evidence:** the game concerns a particular prior selection. The rewrite explains why its repair does not establish a complete rhetorical or philosophical competence.
- **An underused footnote:** footnote 4 now changes the conclusion drawn from the main text, rather than serving as a passing qualification.
- **Missing premises:** the testimonial analysis identifies the teacher's relationship to the pupil and knowledge of the letter's purpose. The words of praise alone do not warrant Grice's inference.
- **Unworked transitions:** each stage supplies the question needed by the next: what writing lacks; which commitment the game requires; why a user infers it; what a record repairs; what remains of Plato's objection.
- **Ornament before structure:** the new title names the topic and authors. The essay uses repeated precise terms and direct explanatory sentences; it adds no decorative internal headings, dividers, or concluding catalogue.
