# W04 — Quote bank and outside sources

**Runner:** the model, with parallel agents if useful. **Reads:** `work/W03_thesis.md`, `work/W01_notes_*.md`, the texts in `context/`, the web. **Writes:** `work/W04_sources.md`; new files into `books/`, with rows added to `books/README.md`.

## Part A — the course texts

For each text the thesis names, draw from the `W01` notes and from the text
itself:

- The crux passages in full, verbatim, with location, and enough surrounding
  text that the paper can quote a sentence and the reader can check the
  paragraph.
- Four to eight further passages, each tagged for the section it will serve
  (*opening*, *claim*, *evidence*, *counter-reading*, *turn*, *close*).
- The edition line the works-cited entry will use.

Every quotation must be findable in the file in `context/` with a plain
search. If a passage in the notes cannot be found again, it does not go in
the bank.

## Part B — the AI papers

For each paper the thesis engages: the exact sentences the paper will cite,
with section and page as printed in the Markdown; the paper's own statement
of its claim; and one sentence on what the citation does for *this*
argument. Do not paraphrase a technical claim you have not read in context.

## Part C — beyond the folder

Three to six outside sources, chosen for one of these roles:

1. A scholarly reading of the course text that the paper agrees or argues with
   (a critical edition's introduction, a well-cited article). Prefer sources
   you can open and quote; if a source can only be cited from its abstract
   or a catalogue record, mark it `[NOT OPENED]`.
2. Nina Beguš, *Artificial Humanities* (2025), if the angle touches it; the
   syllabus names it.
3. One primary document from the AI side the papers point to (a model card,
   a system prompt, a published "constitution") if the angle concerns the
   assistant persona.

Record where each was found and under what licence it may be quoted.
Download legal copies to `books/` and add them to the manifest.

## Part D — the bank

One table, sorted by the section of the paper each entry serves, marking
which quotations are candidates to quote directly and which will be
paraphrased with a citation.

## Rules

- No invented quotations.
- No quotation longer than it needs to be; the paper will quote a sentence,
  the bank holds the paragraph.
- Record the trail so a reader can redo it.
