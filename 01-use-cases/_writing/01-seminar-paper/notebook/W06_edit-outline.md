# W06 — Edit the outline

**Runner:** the model, one adversarial pass. **Reads:** `work/W05_outline.md`, `work/W00_brief.md`, `work/W03_thesis.md`. **Writes:** `work/W06_outline_v2.md`, with a change log of at most ten lines at the top.

## Prompt

> You are the professor reading this outline in office hours. Check, in order:
> 1. Does the opening begin inside a passage, or does it begin with a topic ("Since the advent of AI...")? If a topic, propose the passage.
> 2. Is the claim the same sentence in the opening, the reading, and the close? If it drifts, which version is right?
> 3. Does the reading section actually read, or does it summarize and then assert? For each movement, name the quoted sentence it rests on. If there is none, flag it for `W04`.
> 4. Does the collision change the meaning of the first text, or merely sit beside it? Cut it if it is decoration.
> 5. Is the AI paper cited for what it says? Check the bank entry against the claim the outline attributes to it.
> 6. Is the counter-reading the strongest one, or a convenient one? Propose a stronger one if you can.
> 7. Is any section over budget?
> 8. Is there a reading on the syllabus the professor would expect to see here and does not?
> 9. Does the outline know where it used AI and where it did not? (This becomes `W10`.)
>
> Produce the revised outline and the change log.
