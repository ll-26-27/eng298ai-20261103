# W03 — Thesis

**Chosen by the writer, 2026-11-03:** Angle 1, "One unvarying answer," from `W02_angles.md`. The writer's reason, as given: the recommended angle; the one the syllabus points at.

## 1. Claim

The chatbot does not refute Socrates's objection to writing in the *Phaedrus*; it inverts it. Socrates says the written word fails because, questioned, it "always gives one unvarying answer"; the dialogue agent fails because it cannot give the same answer twice. Both failures have the cause Socrates names at 275e: there is no parent behind the words, no one who could defend them, choose whom to answer, or keep silent.

## 2. Counterclaim

The agent is the written word repaired, not inverted. It answers back, it adapts to the questioner as the conversation goes on (Shanahan §3), and adaptation to the hearer is what Socrates demands of the true rhetorician who knows "the differences of human souls" and "when he should speak and when he should refrain" (271c–272b). The passage this reading stands on is 271c–272b, and Shanahan §3 on the role "extended and/or overwritten" by the user.

## 3. Texts

- **Read closely:** Plato, *Phaedrus* 274c–276a (Theuth and Thamus; writing like painting; the living word), with 271c–272b for the counter-reading and 276b–277a for the close. ## 4. Crux passages

1. **275d–e** (Jowett): "writing is unfortunately like painting … the speaker always gives one unvarying answer … tumbled about anywhere … no parent to protect them." The paper must show that the objection has three parts (cannot be questioned; does not know its hearer; is undefended), and that only the first is about sameness.
2. **276a**: "an intelligent word graven in the soul of the learner, which can defend itself, and knows when to speak and when to be silent." The paper must show that the living word's three marks answer the three parts of the objection, and that silence is one of them.
3. **274e–275b** (Jowett with Fowler): "an aid not to memory, but to reminiscence" / "an elixir not of memory, but of reminding"; "the show of wisdom without the reality" / "the appearance of wisdom, not true wisdom." The paper must show that Thamus's complaint is about where knowing is located, not about whether the writing is true.
4. **Shanahan §5**: regenerate the answer and the agent "will sometimes name an entirely different object." The paper must show that this is 275d's "one unvarying answer" with the sign reversed and the cause unchanged.

## 5. The course question it answers

Seminar 5: "the history of dialogue in order to understand our interactions with AI through conversational interfaces or chats." And Seminar 7: "how does a system that predicts continuations create the impression of a character or self?" The *Phaedrus* gives the first question its oldest form, and the answer to the second, on this reading, is: the way a painting does, with "the attitude of life."
