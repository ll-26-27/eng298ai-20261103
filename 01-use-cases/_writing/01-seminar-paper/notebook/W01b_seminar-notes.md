# W01b — Seminar notes

**Runner: you supply notes; the model can organize or transcribe them.** **Writes:** `work/W01b_seminar-notes.md`.

Your notes from seminar, in your own words, as you took them or typed up
afterward. Dated by session. What was said that you want to keep; what you
disagreed with; the question you did not get to ask; the passage someone
read aloud that landed differently than when you read it alone.

This file brings material from the seminar into the pipeline. `W02` reads it beside the model's reading notes so that the brainstorm
starts from what happened in the room and not only from the texts. If you
skip it, the paper will be about the readings. If you keep it, the paper can
be about the seminar.

Keep it plain. Markdown headings by date are enough. If no notes are
supplied, record that and continue from the readings. Keep any model-generated
discussion questions in a separate, labeled file.
