# W09 — Your feedback

**Runner: you read; the model applies.** **Reads:** `work/W08_draft_v2.md`. **Writes:** `work/W09_feedback.md` (your notes, verbatim), `work/W09_draft_v3.md`.

Read the draft as its author, because you are. Your notes go in as given,
unedited: what is wrong, what you would say differently, the sentence you
want to write yourself, the claim you no longer believe. Write the paragraphs
you want to write; the model does not get to rewrite those.

Then the model applies the notes, lists any note it could not apply and why,
and makes no other change in this pass. If a note conflicts with a judge's
change in `W08`, you win.

This is the step the course's writing unit is about: "to use AI in specific,
intentional ways—and to learn when not to use it." The feedback file is the
record of where you did not.
