# W00 — Brief for the paper

**Runner:** the model. **Reads:** `context/course-readings/syllabus.md`, and any assignment sheet the instructor has posted (put it in `books/` and add it to the manifest). **Writes:** `work/W00_brief.md`.

## Standing note

This course permits every use of AI on condition that it is documented,
explained, and reflected upon. The brief restates that at the top so it
travels with every downstream file. The pipeline is not a way around the
assignment; it is the assignment done in the open.

## What the brief must establish

1. **The deliverable.** Which of the course's written assignments this is
   (the Writing with AI project, 15%; or the written component of the final
   project, 25%), its length, format, due date, and audience. Where the
   syllabus does not say, write the assumption down and mark it **confirm
   with the instructor**.
2. **What the course asks a paper to do.** Reconstruct from the syllabus: the
   "double mission" (how AI uses texts, imitates genres and styles, raises
   questions of creativity and authorship; and a humanities-based perspective
   on AI); the cultural prehistory of AI and its question, "What did these
   writers get right, and what did they get wrong (so far)?"; dialogue as the
   form of our encounter with machines; the fabricated assistant persona; the
   writing unit's aims (AI as sparring partner for refining arguments,
   matching argument to evidence, avoiding logical fallacies).
3. **Audience voice.** A seminar paper for a professor of comparative
   literature who runs the course as a lab. Expect a claim about a text,
   made by reading it, with the passage on the page. Expect the paper to say
   where it used AI and what that did.
4. **Rubric we will judge against** (inferred; confirm with the instructor):
   - Does the paper make a claim about a text, or announce a topic?
   - Is the crux passage quoted, located (Stephanus number, chapter, page),
     and actually read, not summarized?
   - Does the AI question arise *from* the text, rather than being pinned on?
   - Does the paper engage at least one of the AI papers, accurately?
   - Is there a counter-reading the paper answers?
   - Is every use of AI documented, explained, and reflected upon?
   - Prose: exact, light, no throat-clearing.

## Output

One page. The template above with the gaps filled, assumptions marked, and
a dated line at the top saying which assignment this run is for.
