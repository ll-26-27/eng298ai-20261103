# W08 — Judges panel

**Runner:** the model, one reader per judge in parallel, then a synthesis and a revision. **Reads:** `work/W07_draft_v1.md`, `work/W00_brief.md`, the syllabus. **Writes:** `work/W08_judgments.md`, `work/W08_draft_v2.md`.

## The panel

Each judge gets the draft, the brief, and their card. Each returns: a score
on the brief's rubric (1 to 5 per item), the three most important problems
with the line or paragraph where they occur, two things to keep, and one
concrete rewrite of the weakest paragraph. Judges do not see each other.

### Judge 1 — The professor (modeled on the syllabus, not on the person)

Built from the syllabus text only: the double mission; "What did these
writers get right, and what did they get wrong (so far)?"; dialogue as the
form of our encounter with machines; the fabricated assistant and "how a
system that predicts continuations creates the impression of a character or
self"; the writing unit's "sparring partner" for argument and evidence; the
policy that AI use be documented, explained, and reflected upon. Asks: what
is the claim; where is the passage; does the AI question come from the text;
is the paper reading or summarizing; what did the writer do and what did the
model do. Allergic to: topic announcements, "scholars have long debated,"
quotation without reading, AI invoked as a mood rather than a mechanism,
undocumented AI.

### Judge 2 — The philologist

A close reader who checks the text. Asks: is the quotation exact and from
the edition cited; does the paper's gloss match the words; is a translator's
choice being read as the author's; is the Stephanus number right; does the
paper know which *R.U.R.* it is quoting. Allergic to: "Plato says" for what a
translator says; "the text argues" for what one character says; claims about
tone the words do not bear.

### Judge 3 — The stylist

A reader who admires exactness and lightness; Calvino's *Six Memos* virtues
(lightness, quickness, exactitude, visibility, multiplicity) are the house
standard. Asks: is every sentence doing one thing; is the opening passage
vivid; is there a sentence a reader would skim; does the paper end or just
stop. Allergic to: throat-clearing, nominalizations, hedges stacked three
deep, triads that exist for rhythm.

### Judge 4 — (your slot)

A named reader you admire, with the three questions that person always asks.
Leave empty if unused.

## Synthesis

Collate the reports. Where judges disagree, say so and pick. Produce
`W08_draft_v2.md` with a change log of at most fifteen lines at the top. Do
not accept a judge's rewrite wholesale; use it as evidence of the problem.
Record in the judgments file which judge was a model and which, if any, was a
person; `W10` needs this.
