# Q3 — Sketch three outlines

**Runner:** the model sketches; you choose, or don't. **Reads:** `work/Q1_quotes_<slug>.md`, `work/Q2_skeleton_<slug>.md`, the syllabus. **Writes:** `work/Q3_outlines_<slug>.md`.

Three sketches of a paper this text could anchor in this course. Not three
topics: three claims. Each sketch:

1. **Title** (a phrase, not a label).
2. **Claim**, one sentence, something a reader could deny.
3. **Crux passage**, from the bank, by page, with one line on what the paper
   would have to show about it.
4. **Collision**: the other text from the course (a reading or one of the AI
   papers) that changes what the crux means, and the question the collision
   asks. If you have not read the other text in this session, say so and
   mark what it is known for as *to verify*.
5. **Counter-reading**: the strongest objection, with the passage it would
   stand on.
6. **Shape**: five paragraph-level moves, following the order in
   `01-seminar-paper/notebook/W05_outline.md` (open on a passage; read it;
   collide; answer the objection; return).
7. **Fit**, 1 to 5, and the reason.

Then one line: which you would write, and why. The writer may disagree.

Mark the whole file as interpretation. `Q1` and `Q2` can be checked against
the text; this cannot.
