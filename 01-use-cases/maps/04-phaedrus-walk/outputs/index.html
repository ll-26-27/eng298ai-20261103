Walking the Phaedrus

One dialogue, one walk, one afternoon. Socrates and Phaedrus leave Athens by the Olympieion, wade up the Ilissos, and sit under a plane tree to talk about love and writing; then they walk back. The text is aligned to the ground. Solid markers stand on things that are known; dashed ones on conjecture.
