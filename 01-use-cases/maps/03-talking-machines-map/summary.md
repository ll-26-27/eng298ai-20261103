# Where does the machine speak from? · purpose, workflow, method

**Question:** where were talking machines imagined, built, and trained? Not where the stories are set but where the imagining, the building, and the training happened, so that the geography of the dream and the geography of the machine can be seen on one map with a clock.

**Material:** the timeline (maps/02-ai-and-literature-timeline)'s verified timeline (its made-beings lane, its literary course readings, and its conversational machines), five further talking machines with sources, and the 22 AI papers in the course's context pack. 80 rows, 52 places, 750 BCE to 2025.

**Workflow:** code selects the rows → a model, in session, researches one place per row by opening sources and applies a written place rule (composition, else publication or premiere; headquarters for software; first author's printed affiliation for papers), recording certainty and whether the made being speaks → code looks every place up in OpenStreetMap's gazetteer, with Wikidata as a fallback, and accepts only name-verified matches → code embeds the data in a Leaflet page with a year slider, layer and speech filters, and a detail panel.

**Result:** `outputs/index.html`. Drag the slider: the imagined layer spreads from Ionia across Europe and Asia over 2,700 years; the built layer appears in Vienna, Philadelphia, New York, and Cambridge, Massachusetts between 1791 and 1972 and then concentrates; the trained layer sits in about nine cities between 2013 and 2023. Click a place to read every row there with its source and the reasoning for the point.

**Method notes:** the place rule is a convention and the map moves if it changes; five rows are low-certainty placements for legends; 34 rows locate an organisation's headquarters rather than an author's desk; the `speaks` judgment is a reading, not a survey of the texts; the canon is inherited from the timeline (maps/02-ai-and-literature-timeline)'s model-drafted, human-checked timeline and is heavily Anglo-European; the research was done by a model and is recorded row by row so a person can redo it. Coordinates are today's: Ctesiphon is a ruin, and the Ufa-Palast is the Zoo Palast.
