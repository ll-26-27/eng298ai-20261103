Frankenstein's Places

Every named place in the 1818 and 1831 texts of Mary Shelley's Frankenstein, found passage by passage by a language model, counted, and located with a gazetteer. Each passage is tagged with its narrator: Walton's letters hold Victor's story, which holds the Creature's. Click a circle to read the passages. After Franco Moretti's Atlas of the European Novel, 1800–1900.
