You are helping a literary historian build a bibliography of the reception of Mary Shelley's *Frankenstein* (1818; revised 1831).

For each of the following languages, give your best recollection of the **first published translation** of the novel into that language: French, German, Swedish, Dutch, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Catalan, Romanian, Russian, Czech, Hungarian, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Greek, Turkish, Arabic, Hebrew, Bulgarian, Serbian/Croatian.

For each language give the year, translator, title as published, place, publisher, and which English text it followed (1818 or 1831), if you know. Then rate your own confidence (high, medium, low). Use null for anything you do not know. Do not invent precision you do not have: these answers will be checked one by one against library catalogues, and the comparison will be shown to students.
