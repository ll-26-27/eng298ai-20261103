What checking caught

The model's draft was mostly right about years. It was less reliable about which event a date belongs to (a release versus a premiere), about wording in quotations, and about links. These are the corrections.

    How this page was made

    1. Draft (model): a model proposed candidate events from the course reading list and the two histories.
    2. Verify (research): each date and claim was checked against Wikipedia, arXiv, Crossref, publishers, or the text itself, and given a status.
    3. Categorize (model, structured output): track, themes from a fixed list, and a sentence on why it matters.
    4. Build (code): this page, with the data embedded.

    The prompts, scripts, and the full verification table are in the operations/ folder beside this page. Descriptions are paraphrases; quotations appear only for public-domain texts.
